Game Overview

Snake 2034 is a photorealistic survival-action reimagining of classic Snake. Players traverse urban and natural biomes while using living companions and "organic" gear. The tone mixes tense survival with dark humor.

High-level pillars

  • Living Items: gear are bio-entities with needs, stress meters, and emergent behaviour.
  • Traversal & Scale Collision: detailed scale physics for large serpentine allies (design placeholder).
  • Risk-vs-Reward Loot: items give strong benefits with tradeoffs (movement penalties, statuses).
  • Procedural Encounters: bestiary placement blends with environment for surprise mechanics.

Core Mechanics & Systems

  • Companion System — a configurable living rope/pet with stamina, stress, and loyalty meters. Commands: Scout, Shield, Climb.
  • Statuses — Anaconda Squeeze (heavy), Maximum Cute Overload (paralysis), Dirt (attracts insects).
  • Safe Zone — home base with living furniture that heals, saves, and provides crafting stations.
  • Encounters — boss arenas with environment-based mechanics (light management vs Cavern Captain).

Controls (prototype)

WASD / Arrow keys to move, Space to interact, E to command companion, I for inventory, Esc for pause.

Notable Items & Systems

  • Forbidden Royal Fry — large food that restores energy but applies Anaconda Squeeze.
  • Survival Rope — an albino reticulated python used for climbing and scouting; has stress and grip strength.
  • Hissing Comfort Sofa — living couch that heals and saves the game.
  • Hissing Shower 3000 — removes Dirt status via a QTE.

Bestiary Highlights

  • Desert Sheriff — prairie mini-boss, venom & dueling mechanic.
  • Battle Micro-Hisser — swarms that use audio-visual overload to stun.
  • Fake Stick — camouflaged item that steals equipment.
  • Cavern Captain — headlamp-based detection boss in flooded caves; light management is core.
